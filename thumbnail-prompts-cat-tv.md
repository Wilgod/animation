# Meow Meow Thumbnail Prompts — 2-Hour Video (ChatGPT)

Thumbnail for the **Meow Meow** channel's 2-hour cat video (Cat TV style): bugs, lizards, birds, butterflies and fish moving on a garden / grass background.
Paste one prompt into ChatGPT (image generation). Save the result to `image/thumbnail/` with the suggested filename.

## What "Cat TV" thumbnails do (follow these)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **16:9, 1280×720 or larger** | YouTube thumbnail size |
| **Huge, saturated, close-up critters** | Must read at phone size; cats and owners click on bright motion targets |
| **A real cat in the frame** (peeking, paw reaching, watching) | Instantly tells the owner "this video is for my cat" |
| **1–2 words of chunky text max**: `2 HOURS` (no "CAT TV" wording — this is the Meow Meow channel) | Big rounded bold font, yellow/white fill, thick black outline |
| **High contrast, clean background** | Bright green grass makes the critters pop |
| **No clutter, no tiny details** | Thumbnail is viewed at ~200px wide |

**Match the video:** the video is filmed **top-down** (no horizon), so the thumbnail shows the grass from above with the critters on it, and the cat peeking in from the edge. Don't show a perspective / horizon scene.
