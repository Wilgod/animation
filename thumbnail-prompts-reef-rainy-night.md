# Meow Meow Chill Thumbnail Prompts — Underwater Reef, Rainy Day Indoors, Night Garden (ChatGPT)

Thumbnails for three 4-hour Cat TV videos on the **Meow Meow Chill** channel. Same look as the Halloween thumbnail (`image/thumbnail/4 Hours Halloween - Paw Reach.png`): top-down scene, huge bright critters, a real cat, and two lines of text, `4 HOURS` + `CAT TV`.
Paste one prompt into ChatGPT (image generation). Save the result to `image/thumbnail/` with the suggested filename.

## Rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **16:9, 1280×720 or larger** | YouTube thumbnail size |
| **Text: `4 HOURS` (yellow) + `CAT TV` (white on a colored badge)** — nothing else | Same branding across the series |
| **Badge color per theme**: reef = aqua `#00B8D9`, rainy = blue `#2F6FE0`, night = purple `#7B2FBE` | Each video is recognizable at a glance |
| **Huge, saturated, close-up critters** | Must read at ~200px wide |
| **A real cat in the frame** (paw / peeking / watching) | Tells the owner "this video is for my cat" |
| **Top-down, no horizon** | Matches the video |
| **Night and rainy scenes stay bright in the center** | Dark thumbnails disappear in the YouTube feed |
